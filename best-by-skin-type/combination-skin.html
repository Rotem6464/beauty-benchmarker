    Understanding Combination Skin

    The Challenge: Your face has two different skin types. Products for oily skin dry out your cheeks; products for dry skin make your T-zone greasy.

    The Science: Your T-zone has more sebaceous glands, while cheeks have fewer. Hormones, climate, and aging affect this balance.

    The Solution: Adaptable formulas with hydrating yet non-comedogenic ingredients that balance across all zones.

    5 Combination Skin Mistakes

    1

    Mistake:

    Using heavy cream everywhere

    Fix:

    Apply lighter formulas on T-zone, richer ones only where needed (cheeks, under eyes).

    2

    Mistake:

    Over-mattifying the T-zone

    Fix:

    This triggers rebound oiliness. Use balanced formulas, not harsh astringents.

    3

    Mistake:

    Ignoring the cheeks' needs

    Fix:

    Just because T-zone is oily doesn't mean cheeks don't need moisture and lifting.

    4

    Mistake:

    Using two completely different routines

    Fix:

    Find adaptable products that work on both zones—much more practical.

    5

    Mistake:

    Thinking combination is 'normal' skin

    Fix:

    Combination has specific needs. Products for 'normal' skin may not address zone differences.
